Our Story

Affordable Pizza,
Unforgettable Taste

Alberto’s Pizza began as a humble neighborhood pizza shop in Cebu City, just across from the Vicente Sotto Memorial Medical Center. From the very beginning, its founders were committed to one simple goal: serving delicious, high-quality pizzas at prices everyone could afford. By carefully balancing affordability with freshness and quality, they created a pizza experience that quickly won the hearts of their customers.

With its cozy space, Alberto’s Pizza initially focused on takeout and delivery services. But it didn’t take long for word to spread. As more people discovered this hidden gem, customers from all walks of life kept coming back for more. Before long, Alberto’s Pizza had built a loyal following, earning a reputation for its delicious menu, outstanding value, and warm, satisfying dining experience.
